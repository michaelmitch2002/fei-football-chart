What the fit measures

The fit chooses SOR and FEI percentages that add to 100%. Both inputs are independently scaled in standard deviations across all 138 FBS teams in your selected week. For each benchmark and rule, it minimizes MSE = mean((fitted rank − your rank)² / 2) over your 25 teams. Fitted ranks still include all 138 teams; teams you leave out are not assigned a personal rank.

The head-to-head fit makes the same single pass from #1 to #138 as the chart, swapping adjacent teams when the higher team lost to the one below it. It uses all completed games through your selected week. All equally closest benchmark/rule fits are highlighted. Several weights can yield the same ranks; the fit uses the midpoint of the widest best interval when possible. This describes your submitted ranking, rather than judging whether it is correct.
