Weighting

FEI + SOR: score = FEI weight × FEI_std + SOR weight × SOR_std. Each input is centered at its FBS mean and divided by its population standard deviation: standardized value = (value − mean) / SD. Higher scores rank first. The weighted score is not standardized again. FEI represents how you play; projected SOR represents who you play. How the projections and championship choices work

A selected win adds the benchmark’s expected loss probability to SOR. A selected loss adds that probability minus one. For example, a game with 0.30 expected losses changes SOR by +0.30 for a win or −0.70 for a loss. Each team retains its published Week 12 SOR; earlier games are not recalculated.

The page checks possible completions of other conference games against the published qualification rules. A qualification question means at least one completion admits the team and another excludes it. Future computer-rating tiebreakers are left unresolved. Matchup and hosting choices are constrained together; selecting an opponent also assumes the other results needed to make that matchup possible. Changing regular-season results clears affected championship answers and winners.

Expected-loss increments use an approximate reconstruction of published FEI game projections. Holding FEI fixed and adding rounded published SOR introduces approximation. Playoff and bowl games are outside this projection.
