# College Football Schedule and Record Strength

Interactive chart of all 138 FBS teams, with schedule strength, record strength, FEI rating, and a weighted FEI + SOR ranking.

## Data snapshot

- FEI data: selectable Week 11 and Week 12 snapshots for 2026, from https://bcftoys.com/2026-fei
- Week 11 is preserved unchanged. The chart defaults to Week 12; the Data through dropdown changes every metric together.
- Logo selection: AP top 12 and teams with FanDuel make-playoff odds shorter than +1000, based on November 22, 2026 snapshots.
- The chart is a saved snapshot; it does not refresh automatically.

## Weighted ranking

Select **FEI + SOR** and use the slider to choose the FEI weight. Choose an elite, good, or average team benchmark for the SOR input. SOR receives the remaining weight, so weights always sum to one. Both inputs are standardized independently across all 138 FBS teams in the selected week using population standard deviations: `z = (value - mean) / SD`. The score is `w * z(FEI) + (1-w) * z(selected-benchmark SOR)`; higher scores rank first. The weighted score is not standardized again. Scores use published numeric values, and equal scores share a competition rank. The benchmark control is hidden for FEI-only mode.

The combined view contains the score plot for all teams, our top 25 table, the CFP committee top 25 table, and a scatter plot of our rank (x) against committee rank (y). Our table shows rank, logo, team, and combined score; the committee table shows rank, logo, and team. The tables have aligned headers and rows, sit side by side, and stack on narrow screens. The comparison plot uses only team logos as marks, with rank 1 on the right and at the top. Teams outside the committee top 25 are omitted from the comparison plot; they are not assigned an invented committee rank. Verified logo paths for all teams support whichever teams enter our top 25 as weights or benchmarks change.

### Fit weights to committee

FEI and SOR weights are displayed as percentages adding to 100%. Only the selected benchmark's fit and restore button are visible. For each benchmark, the fit minimizes `mean((our rank - committee rank)^2 / 2)` across the 25 committee-ranked teams. This is squared perpendicular distance to the equal-rank line in rank coordinates, independent of chart dimensions. Our ranks always use the complete 138-team population. This is a direct rank-error optimization, not ordinary least squares on raw scores.

Because the objective changes only when teams exchange ranks, the optimizer evaluates every pairwise score crossing, all intervals between crossings, and endpoints 0 and 1. Crossings are evaluated separately because tied competition ranks can have a different loss. If several intervals minimize error, the midpoint of the widest is used; a tie-only optimum uses the crossing itself. Each benchmark has a button to apply or restore its full-precision fitted weight. Slider and saved state retain that precision. Fits are cached separately for each week and unavailable when the matching committee rankings are missing. Results describe the committee release used to fit them.

The November 17, 2026 CFP release (games through November 15) matches the Week 11 snapshot: https://collegefootballplayoff.com/news/2026/11/17/rankings. The November 24, 2026 CFP release, for games through November 21, matches Week 12: https://collegefootballplayoff.com/news/2026/11/24/rankings. Both saved weeks include the committee top 25, rank comparison, and equal-weight and rank-weighted fits with or without head-to-head criteria. FEI-only mode shows standardized values, raw FEI ratings, and published FEI ranks.

## Head-to-head criteria

In FEI + SOR mode, **Apply head-to-head criteria** makes one pass from position 1 toward position 138. At each position, if the lower adjacent team beat the higher adjacent team, they swap; the scan then advances one position. A displaced loser can therefore move down repeatedly. Earlier positions are not revisited, and the pass is not repeated. The rule applies regardless of overall record. All earlier completed games in the selected season through the selected week are eligible; later games are excluded.

The raw weighted scores remain unchanged. With head-to-head enabled, the displayed table and comparison plot use adjusted ordinal positions 1–138. Before the pass, equal scores use alphabetical team order. Turning the option off restores the score-based ranking and its original competition ties.

The separate **Use head-to-head fit** button minimizes the same committee-rank MSE after applying this pass for every candidate weight. It sets the fitted weight and turns head-to-head on. The ordinary fit button applies its score-only optimum and turns head-to-head off. Each benchmark has distinct ordinary and head-to-head optima; the UI shows only the selected benchmark. Both modes require matching committee data.

The cumulative Week 11 and Week 12 result snapshots contain 629 and 695 FBS-vs-FBS games respectively. The full 822-game results archive, including FCS opponents, exactly reproduces all 138 teams' overall records in each saved FEI week. Source: https://cfbplanner.com/games/, retrieved November 24, 2026. Detailed frozen results and provenance are saved in `data/head-to-head-results.json`.

## Personal rankings

`personal.html` lets visitors type or select 25 unique FBS teams using the latest available week automatically (currently Week 12), with no week selector. Drafts stay in browser storage; submission runs locally in a Web Worker. The same chart optimizer fits all three benchmarks in score-only and head-to-head modes against the visitor’s ordered teams, using all 138 FBS teams for standardization and model ranks. All minimum-MSE fits are highlighted; a selector compares each fitted model with the submitted ranking. `personal-data.js` contains frozen copies of the chart’s weekly data and shared mathematical functions. Refresh it whenever the chart’s data or optimizer changes. No server or personal-ranking uploads are used.

## Weighted least squares option

The committee fit and personal ranking page offer equal-weight least squares (the default) or weighted least squares. Target rank r receives weight 1/sqrt(r), applied directly to squared perpendicular rank error. The normalized objective is sum(w_r * (modelRank - r)^2 / 2) / sum(w_r). Committee rank supplies r on the rankings chart; submitted personal rank supplies r on the personal page. Rank 1 receives five times the error weight of rank 25. Both score-only and head-to-head optimizations support this objective for every benchmark. The ranking score itself remains FEI weight * FEI_std + SOR weight * SOR_std.

## Project the finish

`projection.html` offers the 19 remaining regular-season games involving the frozen Week 12 CFP top 15 plus the other logo teams (20 tracked teams total), and their conference championship games. The favorite from a verified published Vegas point spread is automatically selected for every regular-season game and for a championship matchup once it is resolved. Each game labels and links its market source, spread and snapshot date. If no game-specific line is verified for that date and actual venue, the default uses the FEI estimate with an explicit fallback label. The November 25, 2026 BetIQ / TeamRankings snapshot covers all 19 regular-season games and the fixed ACC and Pac-12 championships; other championship scenarios use FEI fallbacks. Conference-title futures are not treated as individual game lines. Odds are a frozen snapshot, not a live feed. Vegas selects default winners only; expected-loss increments still use the FEI approximation. Visitors can override or leave a winner blank; Reset to favorites restores defaults. Favorite identities and benchmark expected losses are precomputed offline. Every selected game updates both participating teams' published Week 12 SOR and overall record. Each selected game adds its benchmark expected losses minus its selected actual loss (0 or 1). Elite, good and average benchmarks use fixed raw FEI ratings 1.2, 0.6 and 0; opponents' Week 12 FEI ratings are held fixed. This estimates future SOR rather than predicting future official FEI recalculations.

The projection page has no top-25 table. Scores and ranks still use all 138 FBS teams. The bubble graphs show only the 20 tracked teams, separated into the frozen Week 12 CFP top 15 and the five Group of 5 contenders. Both retain the horizontal beeswarm format and share one score scale derived from those 20 teams. The team-inspection dropdown and detail row have been removed. The page order is games (regular season and conference championships), benchmark team, weighting and head-to-head, score bubbles, projected playoff bracket with first two teams out. Formulas and benchmark reference ranks match the existing rankings page. Bubble panels use the same framed axes, zero reference, translucent outlined circles, 22px logos, axis labels and vertical packing style as the rankings page. Teams outside the selected games retain their Week 12 values; the page explicitly labels this partial projection. The FEI slider controls `FEI weight * FEI_std + (1-FEI weight) * projected SOR_std`. Both inputs use population standard deviations across the complete 138-team scenario. Head-to-head is enabled by default and uses the same single forward pass as the rankings page, combining historical results through Week 12 with selected future regular-season and valid championship results. It changes model ranks without changing scores or horizontal bubble positions. The graphs pack logo marks vertically to prevent overlap, with native titles containing each team's name, score and rank.

The nine relevant conference championship races are represented by 726 complete combinations of remaining conference results. ACC and Pac-12 matchups are already fixed. Other conferences use clinching scenarios or their published tiebreaker procedures. American and Mountain West computer-rating ties, and Big Ten SportSource ties, remain unresolved: all remaining possible orders are offered as conditional choices. Qualifying questions, matchup choices and campus hosts are constrained together. Changing an affected regular-season result clears dependent championship choices; the calculation also rejects incompatible championship results. Notre Dame has no conference title game. Playoff and bowl games are excluded.

`projection-data.js` contains frozen scenario inputs and precomputed expected-loss lookup outputs. The private probability calibration, its coefficients, inference script and source calibration samples are **not website assets** and must not be uploaded. These outputs are approximate and do not provide secrecy against someone attempting to infer a model from its outputs. The offline builder remains outside this website directory. Browser drafts stay on the visitor's device.

Run `node tests/projection.test.cjs` to check the 726 conference outcome rows, elimination and conditional choices, shared game arithmetic, all three benchmarks, stale championship rejection, favorite defaults and UI events. Tests compare scores and head-to-head order with the existing rankings page for 18 configurations, verify a future upset participates in the pass, confirm there is no top-25 table and verify the 15/5 bubble split, and check shared scales, markup order, removal of the inspector and mobile/desktop geometry. They also verify Vegas favorites and explicit FEI fallback data. Browser visual QA remains separate from these checks.

## Projected playoff bracket

The projection page applies the official 2026 CFP rules to the full model ranking after the selected head-to-head rule. The ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions receive bids, along with the highest-ranked team from the other six conferences (a conference championship is not required for that fifth bid in 2026). Remaining places go to the highest-ranked unselected teams. Notre Dame is guaranteed inclusion when in the model's top 12. The field has 12 unique teams, seeded in model order; seeds 1–4 get byes and 5–8 host 12–9. The bracket follows the fixed quarterfinal and semifinal paths without reseeding. Later playoff winners are placeholders, not simulated outcomes. First two out means the two highest-ranked teams excluded from the selected field after automatic bids, not necessarily model ranks 13 and 14.

Required power-conference championship matchups and winners must be resolved before the bracket and cut line are populated. An unresolved required championship displays a clear message and bracket placeholders; it never silently assumes a champion. Other unselected future games retain the existing partial-scenario behavior. Selection rules and dates are specific to 2026, from https://collegefootballplayoff.com/sports/2016/10/24/selection-committee-protocol?hl=en-US. Tests cover champion bids outside the model top 12, the Notre Dame guarantee, seeding, first two excluded teams, unresolved fields and the displayed round structure.

The playoff field and first two out are restricted to the 20 tracked contenders. Ole Miss and all other outside teams remain in the 138-team scoring and ranking population but cannot enter this contender-only bracket, including via an automatic bid. An outside conference champion is excluded without reassigning its championship to another team. Automatic bids and the highest-ranked Group of Six place are applied within the eligible pool; remaining places follow model order. The page labels this restriction explicitly.
